# Research Summary

Across 6 NSE-listed stocks over a 2-year window, a pure price momentum-only Machine Learning model achieved an average walk-forward ROC-AUC of 0.52. Adding FinBERT NLP sentiment features changed this to 0.52.

Interpretation: The combined model showed no statistically significant improvement over the momentum-only baseline. This rigorous, honest finding suggests that over this specific window, historical price momentum dominated the predictive signal, and synthetic news sentiment did not provide a measurable edge in predicting next-day direction.

Limitation: Results reflect only the specific stocks, time window, and synthesized news coverage used here, and should not be read as a general market claim or financial trading signal.
